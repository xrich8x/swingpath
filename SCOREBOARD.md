# SCOREBOARD.md - moved

**Split on 2026-08-26 into two files, because it was doing two jobs.**

- **[docs/STATE.md](docs/STATE.md)** - the living record: the stack, what has and has
  not moved a number, what is open. Every row names its evidence file. **This is the
  file to update in the same commit as your work**, and the one the commit hook checks.
- **[docs/evidence/](docs/evidence/)** - one file per result: the mechanism, the war
  story, the caveats and the retraction narrative. Nothing was discarded in the split;
  every row's original text is preserved verbatim in its evidence file.
- **[ML_PRACTICES.md](ML_PRACTICES.md)** - the 11 working rules that used to live here
  under "The method". They are discipline, not state.
- **[TRAPS.md](TRAPS.md)** - unchanged, and never renumbered.
- **[docs/archive/resolved/](docs/archive/resolved/)** - Open rows that have been
  answered, kept as pointers rather than in the live table.

This stub stays so that older commits, links and citations still land somewhere that
tells you where to go. Do not add content here.
