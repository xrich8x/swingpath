What you are doing

Each item loops about half a second of match video. Only judge a bounce whose ground contact happens while the picture has a GREEN border (the green "target zone" on the bar). Ignore anything outside it - a neighbouring item covers that. Some items contain nothing at all; that is by design. Answer from what you see (and, on the clip that has it, hear). "Cannot tell" is a real answer - use it rather than guessing.

Q1 - Is there a ball bounce (ground contact) inside the green zone?

A hidden bounce still counts if it is evident: the ball falls into a player/the net and comes back up, or you hear it. A racquet hit is NOT a bounce. Only IN-PLAY bounces count - from a serve or a shot, until the point ends. Answer "no" for a server bouncing the ball before serving, a ball being fed, knocked back or collected between points, and a ball from another court.

Q2 - At the contact moment (give or take one frame), can you see the ball well enough to say where it touched?

VISIBLE, or the cause it is hidden: PLAYER (body or racquet) - NET (net, tape or post) - FRAME (outside the picture) - BLUR (there, but blurred or too small to recognise) - OTHER (say what: fence, umpire, ball kid, graphic...). Partly hidden but you can still place the contact point = VISIBLE.

Q3 - Did it land within about 0.5 m of any painted line?

Any line: both pairs of sidelines (doubles alley counts), baselines, service lines, centre service line. 0.5 m is about a third of the doubles-alley width, or a bit less than a racquet's length. If the ball was hidden you may still answer from where it went in and came out; otherwise "cannot tell".

Q4 - Which half of the court?

NEAR = the camera's side of the net.

Grey boxes

Scoreboards and the SwingVision panels (its mini-court shows its own bounce dots) are covered on purpose, so that nobody else's call leaks into your answer. If the ball is behind a grey box at contact, answer OTHER and write "graphic".

Pace

About 15 s an item. Batch 1 = items P2-001 to P2-180 (~45 min). Stop at the end of a batch, not in the middle, if you can. Answers save automatically in this browser; press Download answers when you finish (and whenever you stop) and send the CSV to qa.
