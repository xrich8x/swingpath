# 60 fps shipped as --full-rate

> Evidence for the `60-fps-shipped-as-full-rate` row in [docs/STATE.md](../STATE.md) (What has worked).
> Text preserved verbatim from SCOREBOARD.md at the 2026-08-26 split.

**A measured lever that had no product surface.** `--frame-step 1` has always processed every frame, so the MECHANISM was never missing — but as a bare integer nothing said it is the largest measured accuracy gain in the tool, or what it costs. The decision (Session H parts 5-6, `data/output/fps_decision.md`) had sat in Open as "a product call, not more measurement" while remaining un-called. Now `--full-rate` states the trade in its own help: **+5.8 pts close-call accuracy at a 1.5 m mount, arc reproj 148 → 91 px, HUD speed error 38.9 → 33.1%, bounce error −24..−35%**, against **2× perception time** and a **wash-to-negative on DETECTION** — and prints the same at runtime, because a mode that doubles runtime must announce it. Mutually exclusive with `--frame-step` (passing both is a contradiction, not a preference). **Default unchanged at `auto`** — opt-in until a full match has been run at full rate. Verified end to end: yt_rally2 (60 fps) goes `frame_step=2 → 1`, demo30 (29 fps) reads `frame_step=1` **either way**, so the "no-op on 30fps" claim in the help is measured rather than asserted. Cache invalidation needed no work — `frame_step` was already stamped and checked, so the two rates cannot be silently mixed. 6 tests, 387 total | 2026-08-15
