# Depth-invariant static-player guard (body_relative)

> Evidence for the `depth-invariant-static-player-guard` row in [docs/STATE.md](../STATE.md) (What has not worked).
> Text preserved verbatim from SCOREBOARD.md at the 2026-08-26 split.

**GATE FAILS, 1 of 3 calibrated clips, and the failure relocates the problem.** Part 1 showed `--far-player-rescue` reaching 37.2% far coverage on yt_rally2 and `_reject_static_player` deleting all of it via radii written for a near player; `body_relative=True` judges motion in fractions of the track's own body height instead. Pre-registered bar was **up on >=2 of 3 calibrated clips**. Result: **yt_rally2 0.0 -> 28.2 (yes), yt_match40 0.0 -> 0.0, am_hard_utr 0.0 -> 0.0**. Near coverage identical on all three (90.8 / 83.4 / 74.2), so nothing was at risk on the near side. It is directionally right everywhere — it always drops fewer frames (am_hard_utr 3,699 -> 3,303) — and only changes the OUTCOME once. **The two misses fail for different reasons and neither is the radius bug:** yt_match40 has the far player on only **14.5%** of frames, already under the guard's own 15% floor, so a perfect filter changes nothing; am_hard_utr has 26.7% and the filter still leaves 573, under its 2,174 floor. **So on the target footage the far player is a DETECTION problem, not a filtering one** — Part 1's "the lever works and a guard cancels it" is true on yt_rally2 and misleading as a general claim. Kept off-by-default with the numbers in the docstring. Second independent reason not to ship: the fixture population the guard exists to catch appears in **no gold clip**, so that leg of the gate is unmeasurable on real data.
